Milestone 8, Section 8.10. These forms are templates for capstone usability and
user-satisfaction review. Do not collect private credentials, cookies, student
records, or production data in survey responses.

## Task Script

Ask the participant to complete the relevant tasks for their role:

1. Sign in or continue as the assigned demo role.
2. Find a campus building with map search.
3. Open route/pathfinding instructions for a known destination.
4. Open a VR scene/route and move through at least one hotspot.
5. View announcements/events relevant to the role.
6. Test offline fallback or observe the facilitator's offline demonstration.
7. Admin only: create, edit, and delete one demo content item.

Record only pass/fail, time, and non-sensitive observations.
